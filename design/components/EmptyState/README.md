An empty state explains why there is nothing and offers the next step.

## Use
- `rj-empty` inside the panel it replaces: `rj-empty__title` (what happened), `rj-empty__body` (the numbers and the most useful change), `rj-empty__actions` (one primary action, one secondary).

## Rules
- Use real numbers from the scan: how many companies were checked and what a change would add.
- No illustrations, mascots or emoji. Text and actions only.
- Left-aligned, at most 52ch wide.
